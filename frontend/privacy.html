Privacy Policy

Automated Sermon Publisher (ASP)

What This Application Does

The Automated Sermon Publisher (ASP) helps church volunteers upload, process, and publish sermon recordings. It processes video files and uploads them to YouTube and Google Drive on behalf of the church.

Data We Collect

ASP collects only the data you provide through the upload form:

  • Sermon title, date, and speaker name
  • Video file(s) of the sermon recording
  • Optional: series name, trim start/end times

We do not collect personal information about visitors or volunteers beyond what is needed to process the upload.

How We Use Your Data

  • Video files are temporarily stored in Google Cloud Storage during processing
  • Processed videos are uploaded to the church's YouTube channel
  • Processed videos are archived to the church's Google Drive
  • Temporary files are automatically deleted after processing completes

Google API Usage

This application uses Google APIs (YouTube Data API v3 and Google Drive API) to publish and archive sermon recordings. The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data Retention

  • Upload staging files: deleted automatically within 7 days
  • Job metadata: retained for 30 days for troubleshooting, then deleted
  • Published YouTube videos: retained per the church's YouTube channel policies
  • Google Drive archives: retained per the church's Drive storage policies

Third-Party Services

  • Google Cloud Platform (processing infrastructure)
  • YouTube (video publishing)
  • Google Drive (video archiving)
  • GitHub Pages (frontend hosting)

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or to request data deletion, contact the church administration.
